## 1. Definitions

**Project** means the hReader software, documentation, assets, tests, build
files, models or integrations identified by the Project Owner, including later
versions and successor repositories designated by the Project Owner.

**Contribution** means an original work of authorship, or an identified part
of a work, that the Contributor intentionally submits to the Project Owner for
possible inclusion in the Project. A Contribution may be submitted through a
pull request, commit, patch, issue attachment, code review system, or another
channel designated by the Project Owner. A submission explicitly marked
`NOT A CONTRIBUTION` is excluded.

This Agreement applies only to Contributions accepted or incorporated by the
Project Owner. A rejected contribution is not assigned unless the parties sign
a separate written agreement.

## 2. Assignment of copyright

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Contributor hereby
assigns and transfers to the Project Owner all worldwide right, title, and
interest in and to each accepted Contribution, including all transferable
copyright and related rights, for the entire term of those rights and all
renewals, extensions, and revivals.

The assignment includes, in every field of exploitation permitted by law:

- reproduction in any medium, including source, object code, APK, AAB, archive,
  backup, and printed form;
- distribution, sale, rental, lending, import, export, and making available;
- public performance, display, communication, and online hosting;
- adaptation, modification, translation, arrangement, combination, porting,
  and creation of derivative works;
- publication through app stores, package repositories, websites, source
  repositories, and network services;
- sublicensing, relicensing, commercial licensing, and granting exceptions;
- use in proprietary, open-source, source-available, or dual-licensed versions
  of the Project.

The Project Owner may exercise these rights alone or through a company,
successor, assignee, licensee, or sublicensee designated by the Project Owner.

## 3. No obligation to publish or license

The Project Owner is not required to accept, publish, distribute, maintain,
attribute, or license any Contribution. The Project Owner may release the
Project or any version of it under GNU AGPLv3-only, another open-source
license, a source-available license, a proprietary license, or multiple
licenses, subject to the rights of third parties whose material is included.

## 4. Moral rights and similar rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Contributor waives moral rights

worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, non-exclusive patent license

This patent grant terminates for a party that brings patent litigation alleging
that the Contribution or the Project infringes a patent, to the extent allowed

## Implementation policy for the repository

Before accepting external code, the Project Owner should:

1. collect a signed copy through a private channel;
2. record the contributor identity and the covered repository/project scope;
3. check employer or corporate ownership;
4. verify third-party license declarations;
5. link the accepted contribution to the stored agreement; and
6. only then merge the contribution.

There is no obvious-fix, typo, bot or AI waiver to this agreement. If an
external person's work is accepted into the repository, the person or actual
rights-holding entity must sign before merge. This agreement is intentionally
an assignment rather than a copyright-retaining CLA. It is therefore stronger
for relicensing control, but it also requires careful identity verification,
jurisdiction-specific wording and a private record-keeping process.
