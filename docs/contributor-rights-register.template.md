## Required private evidence

- signed agreement in the version recorded above;
- identity and signature verification;
- employer, client, school or grant authorization when applicable;
- exact accepted PR, commit range or patch scope;
- third-party and generated-material declarations;
- any replacement, termination or dispute note.

Do not put legal names, addresses, email addresses, signatures, scans,
employment contracts or confidential correspondence in this repository,
GitHub issues, pull requests or commit messages.
